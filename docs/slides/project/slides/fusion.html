Weights chosen on 10 dev pairs; reported as a sensitivity check

Our hybrid merges dense search and keyword search with equal weight. For the strong model this pulled results down: on negation questions bge-m3 alone gets 0.79 in Russian and 0.97 in Kazakh, the hybrid 0.74 and 0.89. When we gave keyword search a quarter of the weight, the hybrid was never below dense search, but also not clearly above it. Because the weight was tuned on only ten development question pairs, we present this as a sensitivity check, not as a new result.
