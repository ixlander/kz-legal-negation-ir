The problem

Dense search ranked the opposite norm 5th and the correct norm 99th

Question

Do I pay personal income tax when I sell an apartment I have owned for more than two years?

Opposite norm art. 383(1)

Income arises when housing owned for less than two years is sold

5th

Correct norm art. 382(5)

No income arises when housing owned for two years or more is sold

99th

bge-m3 dense ranking over all 22 codes · Russian test question

Here is the failure we care about. A person asks whether they pay income tax after selling an apartment they owned for more than two years. The correct answer is in article 382: no income arises, so no tax. But the dense search model puts a different paragraph 5th: the one saying income does arise if you sold within two years. The correct paragraph is 99th. Stress those two numbers. A chatbot that reads only the top 5 results sees just the wrong rule and will answer that tax is due. In Kazakh the same problem may be worse, because negation is a verb suffix: төлейді means pays, төлемейді means does not pay.
