Research question

Do a morphological BM25 and an NLI reranker retrieve the article with the right polarity, and do they help Kazakh more than Russian?

Hypotheses fixed before the evaluation set was finished

This is the question we set out to answer. Polarity means whether a rule allows or forbids something. We tested two cheap ideas. First, a keyword search, BM25, that understands word forms and keeps the word "not" as a separate signal. Second, a reranker based on natural language inference, a model trained to say whether one sentence follows from another or contradicts it. We also asked whether these help Kazakh more than Russian, because Kazakh hides negation inside the word. We wrote our hypotheses down before the evaluation questions were finished, so the results could not shape them.
