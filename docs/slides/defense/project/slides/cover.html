Do retrievers confuse “may” and “may not”?

Negation-aware retrieval over the codes of Kazakhstan, in Russian and Kazakh

Maxim Abakarov · Balym Sekenova

Kazakh-British Technical University · github.com/ixlander/kz-legal-negation-ir

Good afternoon. Our project is about legal search in Russian and Kazakh. When someone asks whether something is allowed, the search engine has to bring back the article that answers it, and often there is a neighbouring article on the same topic that says the opposite. We built a benchmark on the real codes of Kazakhstan, measured how often retrievers make this mistake, and tested two components that were supposed to fix it, including an NLI reranker that we fine-tuned. Everything we show comes from scripts in the repository, run on a held-out test split.
