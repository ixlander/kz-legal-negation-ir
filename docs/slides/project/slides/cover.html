Research project · Final defense · December 2026

Do retrievers confuse "may" and "may not"?

A parallel Russian and Kazakh benchmark for negation in legal retrieval

Maxim Abakarov · Balym Sekenova

Kazakh-British Technical University

Our project asks a simple question about legal search. When a person asks whether something is allowed, does the search engine bring back the article that says yes or the one that says no? These two articles often differ by one word, and search models are known to be weak at that. We studied it for Russian and Kazakh, on the real codes of Kazakhstan. Kazakh is interesting because negation sits inside the verb as a suffix. The short version of our answer: the problem exists but is smaller than we expected for a strong model, and the two fixes we proposed did not help.
