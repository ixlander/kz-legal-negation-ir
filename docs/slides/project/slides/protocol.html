We want to be open about how the evaluation data was checked, because it limits what our numbers mean. An LLM drafted the questions, and our teammate reviewed them while seeing the drafts. All accepted questions went through without a single edit, and the save times suggest about seventeen seconds per question. Three of the NLI labels she changed look wrong on re-reading, so we also report results without them. We designed parts of the NLI reranker on these same questions before splitting off a test set. Finally, the Russian tax questions often use abbreviations that the law never uses, while the Kazakh versions spell the terms out, which makes the language comparison less clean.
