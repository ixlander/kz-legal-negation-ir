Limitations and next steps

What this study cannot show

Limitations

  • 19 or 20 test questions per slice: only differences of about 0.2 are detectable
  • Negation questions are all yes/no, general ones all wh-questions
  • One annotator, who saw the drafts; no agreement statistic
  • One gold per question; one training seed per NLI variant

Next steps

  • 150 or more questions with a balanced design: permitting golds, explicit negation in the question
  • A blind second annotator and relevance judgments for every system's top 5
  • Questions with abbreviations; a query expansion step for them
