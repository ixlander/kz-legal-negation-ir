Here is the scorecard. D1 holds against a full paraphrase, but against a fair one-word edit the models do separate negation, so we report it as a weak signal rather than a failure. D2 holds as a description. Our two fixes failed: keyword search did not shrink the gap, and the NLI reranker never beat the ready-made rerankers. Kazakh gained more than Russian only for the weaker model, and on all questions. H5 cannot be tested because the base model already saw Turkish NLI data. For H6, the counter-examples cut false contradictions on synthetic pairs from 36 percent to 1 percent, but we had too few hand-written pairs to confirm it.
