3 · Architecture

A retrieve-then-rerank pipeline: each configuration adds one component

B0 · dense

bge-m3 bi-encoder, cosine over 30,766 vectors (1024-d)

BM25 · lexical

lemmas / stems + NEG, k1 1.5, b 0.75

B2 · hybrid

reciprocal rank fusion, k = 60

B1 = same with plain BM25

B3′ · baseline reranker

bge-reranker-v2-m3 or jina-v2 cross-encoder

B3-rule · baseline

±α if question and paragraph polarity match

B3 · ours

fine-tuned NLI cross-encoder

Rerankers reorder the top 30 of B2 · no generation step · e5-base repeated as a weaker dense model

The architecture is a standard retrieve-then-rerank pipeline. B0 encodes the question and every paragraph with bge-m3 and ranks by cosine similarity. BM25 does the same lexically on lemmas with the negation token. B2 fuses the two rankings with reciprocal rank fusion. Then the top thirty are reordered in one of three ways: an off-the-shelf cross-encoder, which is our main baseline; a simple polarity rule; or our fine-tuned NLI model. Why not something simpler: BM25 alone and dense alone are both in the table as baselines. Why not a larger model: the point is to fix the evidence cheaply, before generation.
