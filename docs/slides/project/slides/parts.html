What we built

We built a benchmark, four metrics and two fixes with controls

1

Benchmark

Parallel Russian and Kazakh text of 22 codes. 49 question pairs, each with a gold paragraph and, if one exists, an opposite-polarity twin.

2

Metrics

Two encoder diagnostics: Negation Margin and Affix Isolation Rate. Two retrieval metrics: Negation Gap and Twin-above-Gold.

3

Two cheap fixes

BM25 that keeps negation as a token, and a fine-tuned NLI reranker. Controls: two off-the-shelf rerankers and a polarity rule.

Questions on the Labor and Tax Codes of Kazakhstan

The project has three parts. First, a benchmark: the same 22 codes in Russian and Kazakh, and 49 questions asked in both languages. For each question we marked the correct paragraph, the gold, and where possible a twin: a paragraph on the same topic that says the opposite. Second, metrics. Twin-above-Gold is the key one: how often the wrong twin ranks above the correct paragraph. Third, two cheap fixes, compared against controls that need no training: ready-made rerankers and a simple rule that boosts paragraphs whose polarity matches the question.
