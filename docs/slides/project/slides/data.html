All evaluation data is our own. We took every code of Kazakhstan whose Russian and Kazakh versions have the same revision, 22 codes, and split them into paragraphs: about thirty thousand per language. Questions are only about the Labor and Tax Codes; the other twenty codes are distractors. An LLM drafted the questions and other texts, and our Kazakh-speaking teammate checked all of it: 49 of 50 questions were accepted, 5 of 30 Kazakh triples rewritten, 6 percent of NLI labels changed, 7 of 18 flagged alignments rejected. She also found an error in the source: in article 26 of the Kazakh Labor Code, points 1 and 2 are in reverse order.
