Here is the scorecard. The two diagnostics held: models put negations close, and the tokenizer rarely isolates the Kazakh suffix, though the first one needs the fairer comparison we showed. Our main hypotheses about the fixes failed: keyword search did not shrink the gap between negation and general questions, and the NLI reranker never beat the ready-made rerankers. Kazakh gained more than Russian only for the weaker model, and on all questions, not just negation ones. We call a difference real only when its 95 percent interval excludes zero.
