We read the twenty test questions that our systems missed most often. Only four are the polarity errors the project is about; the largest group is simply a weak model on Kazakh, and the rest are ordinary retrieval problems such as abbreviations. On the right is one question traced through the systems. Dense search gets it right. The hybrid pulls up the article that lists when an employer may dismiss, and the correct prohibition drops to twelfth. The off-the-shelf reranker brings it back to second; our NLI reranker leaves it at twelfth. To investigate a failure like this we look at the NLI probabilities for both paragraphs, which the demo shows.
