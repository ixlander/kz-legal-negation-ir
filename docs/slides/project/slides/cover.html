Do retrievers confuse "may" and "may not"?

A parallel Russian and Kazakh benchmark for negation in legal retrieval

[Author names] · December 2026

This project asks whether search over the codes of Kazakhstan confuses a norm with its negation, and whether two cheap components fix it. Short answer: the problem is smaller than we expected for a strong retriever, and our two fixes did not help; off-the-shelf rerankers did.
