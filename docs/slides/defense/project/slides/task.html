1 · Problem and NLP task

Task: rank the paragraph of law that answers a question, with the right polarity

Task type

Passage retrieval (ranking), Russian and Kazakh

Input

A legal question: “May an employer dismiss a pregnant employee due to staff reduction?”

Output

Ranking of 30,766 paragraphs of 22 codes, with scores

Target

Annotated gold paragraph; for polarity questions also a twin with the opposite rule

Success

Gold in the top 5; twin never above gold

Why it matters · bge-m3, test question

“Is income from selling an apartment owned for more than two years taxed?”

twin #5

“income arises if sold within two years”

gold #99

“no income arises”

Users: builders of legal RAG assistants; the top 5 becomes the LLM’s context

Our task is retrieval. The input is a question in Russian or Kazakh; the output is a ranking of all thirty thousand paragraphs of twenty-two codes. Each question has an annotated gold paragraph, and questions whose answer depends on polarity also have a twin: a paragraph on the same topic with the opposite rule. Success means the gold is in the top five and the twin is not above it. On the right is a real test case: dense search put the opposite rule fifth and the correct one ninety-ninth, so an assistant reading the top five would answer wrongly.
