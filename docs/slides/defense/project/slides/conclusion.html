6 · Conclusion

Polarity errors exist but are rare for a strong retriever; use an off-the-shelf reranker first

Main result

bge-m3 ranks the twin above the gold for 2 of 14 Ru and 1 of 14 Kz questions; a cross-encoder brings this to 0–1. Our two components do not beat it.

Limitations

19–20 test questions per cell; one annotator; one seed per NLI variant; negation questions are all yes/no.

Next steps

150–200 balanced questions with a second annotator; a negation signal bound to its predicate; real user questions.

Report, code, data, results: github.com/ixlander/kz-legal-negation-ir

To sum up. We built a parallel Russian–Kazakh benchmark on real codes and measured the polarity error directly. For a strong dense model it happens, but rarely: two of fourteen Russian and one of fourteen Kazakh questions. An off-the-shelf cross-encoder reranker reduces it further, and our two components, the negation token in BM25 and the fine-tuned NLI reranker, do not beat that baseline. The main limitation is size: about twenty test questions per cell. Next we would grow the set with balanced questions and a second annotator. Thank you; we are ready for questions.
