Accuracy · Kardeş 1,000 pairs · manual 50 per language · synthetic 351

We fine-tuned the NLI model in four variants. On our rule-made synthetic test, accuracy jumps from 0.68 to about 0.97. But on hand-written pairs built from real Kazakh norms, every fine-tuned variant is below the base model, 0.56, though no difference is statistically clear. The model learned the templates of our generator, not legal reasoning. Two hypotheses sit here. H5, Turkish data: variants a and b do not differ, and the base model already saw translated Turkish and Russian NLI, so this test is weak. H6, counter-examples: on synthetic range pairs the base model wrongly says contradiction 36 percent of the time, our model 1 percent, and the model trained without counter-examples 61 percent, so they do help there. On 8 hand-written Kazakh range pairs both fine-tuned variants make no such errors, so we cannot credit the counter-examples.
