Research question

Do a morphological BM25 and an NLI reranker retrieve the article with the right polarity, and do they help Kazakh more than Russian?

Benchmark

Parallel Russian and Kazakh text of 22 codes. 49 question pairs on the Labor and Tax Codes, each with a gold paragraph and, if one exists, an opposite-polarity twin.

Metrics

Two encoder diagnostics: Negation Margin and Affix Isolation Rate. Two retrieval metrics: Negation Gap and Twin-above-Gold.

Two cheap fixes

BM25 that keeps negation as a token, and a fine-tuned NLI reranker. Controls: two off-the-shelf rerankers and a polarity rule.

Twin-above-Gold is the share of polarity-sensitive questions where the best twin ranks above the best gold paragraph in the full ranking. The hypotheses were fixed before the evaluation set was finished.
