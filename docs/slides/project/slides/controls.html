Why the two fixes did not work

Negation token: no effect

BM25 with and without the negation tokens gives the same results in 3 of 4 cells. Only 2 of 24 questions contain a negation word, so the token has nothing to match. The BM25 gain for e5-base on Kazakh comes from lemmatization.

Equal-weight fusion hurts

With equal weights the hybrid is below bge-m3 alone. With BM25 weight 0.25 it is never below B0, and not significantly above it.

Fine-tuning adds nothing

The NLI model without our fine-tuning gives the same reranking: mean Recall@5 0.80 vs 0.81. Both are below B2 (0.85). Rewriting questions as statements lowers it to 0.70.

The NLI reranker judges whether a statement follows from a premise. A yes or no question about a topic is not such a statement, so its scores reorder relevant chunks by something other than relevance.
