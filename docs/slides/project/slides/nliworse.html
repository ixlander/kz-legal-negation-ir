Our NLI reranker put more twins above the gold than an off-the-shelf reranker

+0.29

Twin-above-Gold of the NLI reranker over bge-reranker, bge-m3, Russian: 4 questions, all in one direction

Bootstrap 95% CI [0.07, 0.54] · exact sign test p = 0.125

Partly built in: the selected score P(e) − P(c) penalises prohibiting golds

With bge-m3 on Russian, our NLI reranker puts the wrong twin above the correct paragraph more often than the ready-made bge reranker, by 0.29. The bootstrap interval excludes zero, but the difference comes from four questions, and an exact test gives p of 0.125, so we call it suggestive. Part of it is built into the score we selected: entailment minus contradiction. Most correct answers are prohibitions, and a prohibition contradicts a positively worded question, so this score pushes correct answers down. Even the best NLI variant on test stays below the ready-made reranker, so the conclusion that our reranker does not beat it holds.
