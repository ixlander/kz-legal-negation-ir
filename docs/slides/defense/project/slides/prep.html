2 · Preprocessing and tokenization

Two text paths: subwords for the neural models, lemmas with a negation token for BM25

Neural path · bge-m3, rerankers, NLI

SentencePiece subwords: XLM-R vocabulary 250,002 (bge-m3), mDeBERTa 250,102. Article title prepended to each paragraph. Dynamic padding per batch, attention mask, truncation at 512 (retrieval), 128 (NLI training), 256 (NLI scoring).

төлемейді → ▁төл · е · мейді
болмайды → ▁болмайды (one token)
XLM-R isolates the affix in 21% of 171 forms

Lexical path · BM25

Ru: lowercase, pymorphy3 lemmas, stop words removed except не, ни, нет, без; не- prefix split off. Kz: kazsearch stems, negative verb → stem + NEG, емес and жоқ kept.

неуплата налога → не уплата налог
бұза алмайды → бұза ал NEG

Paragraph = chunk; long paragraphs split by sub-points · no other normalization

The text takes two paths. For the neural models we keep the text as is and let the SentencePiece tokenizer split it into subwords; batches are padded dynamically with an attention mask, and long paragraphs are truncated. For BM25 we normalize: Russian words become lemmas, Kazakh words become stems, and we keep negation explicit. Russian stop words are removed except the negation words, and in Kazakh a negative verb becomes its stem plus a NEG token. Our tokenizer diagnostic showed that XLM-R separates the Kazakh negative affix in only twenty-one percent of forms, which motivated the NEG token.
