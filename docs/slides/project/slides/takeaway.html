What to take away

Put a cross-encoder over the dense top 30 before building anything negation-specific.

On this benchmark a strong retriever confuses polarity in 1 or 2 of 14 questions, and an off-the-shelf reranker removes most of the rest at no training cost.

If you remember one thing, make it this. For legal search in Russian and Kazakh, a strong dense model already gets polarity wrong only in one or two of fourteen questions. A ready-made cross-encoder reranker, applied to the top 30 results, fixes most of what is left and needs no training. So before building anything special for negation, add that reranker. Also note the Kazakh surprise: the tokenizer result did not carry over to search, and the help keyword search gave the weak Kazakh model came from word forms, not negation.
