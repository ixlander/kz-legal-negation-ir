Here we asked whether our fine-tuning mattered. The NLI model before our fine-tuning reranks just as well as after: 0.80 against 0.81 average Recall@5. Both are below the hybrid they rerank, 0.85. We also tried turning each yes or no question into a statement, since NLI models are trained on statements; that made things worse, 0.70. The likely reason: an NLI model judges whether a statement follows from a text, and a question about a topic is not a statement, so its scores reorder good candidates by something other than relevance.
