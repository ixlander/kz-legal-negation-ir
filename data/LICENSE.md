# Data license

* `data/corpus/`: official texts of the codes of the Republic of Kazakhstan, taken from adilet.zan.kz (Institute of
  Legislation and Legal Information of the Republic of Kazakhstan). The split into paragraphs and the alignment are
  ours. Check the source's terms before redistributing the legal texts themselves.
* Everything else in `data/` that we created (evaluation questions with gold and twin ids, D1 triples, D2 form
  annotations, the hand-written NLI validation pairs, the rule-made synthetic NLI pairs, annotation answers, error
  notes) is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please cite the work as given in the README.
* Third-party datasets used by the code are not included; they keep their own licenses.
